# MS1: evidence-backed comparison and outreach to Proxima Fusion

Planning framework, 23 September 2026. **Not reached.** This is not a numerical
study protocol, a result, or an assertion about which design Proxima currently pursues.
[Project roadmap](../PROJECT_PLAN.md) · [Current evidence](../STATUS.md)

## Purpose and trigger

The end goal is to contribute to nuclear fusion for humanity by finding the best
reactor design current technology can achieve. MS1 is an intermediate milestone:
as soon as strong, reproducible and independently checked evidence supports that
a design we found is better than the design Proxima Fusion is pursuing, we will
contact them with the evidence and invite technical scrutiny.

This is a targeted outcome of roadmap Step 5. It is not a renaming of Step 1, a
reward for a software release, proof of global optimality, or the end of research.
It does not require that a reactor has already been built, but computational
claims must remain within the demonstrated validity of their physics/engineering
models. Expert discussion and further validation follow; endorsement is not assumed.

## What strong evidence must establish

| Requirement | Evidence to prepare |
| --- | --- |
| Correct comparison target | An authoritative, versioned design/data package, provenance and a dated check that it represents the design Proxima is pursuing. Do not assume SQuID-C or an old publication still does. |
| Reproduced baseline | Executable reconstruction and relevant reference metrics reproduced within justified tolerances. Missing inputs or mismatches are explicit gaps, not permission to substitute our own easier target. |
| Like-for-like comparison | Matched or explicitly justified size, field, operating conditions, plasma assumptions and technology/engineering constraints; disclose every material difference. |
| Meaningful advantage | Predeclared reactor-relevant objectives and hard feasibility gates. Quantify the advantage and all trade-offs; explain why no regression invalidates the design-level conclusion. |
| Credible uncertainty and robustness | Resolution/model checks and relevant manufacturing/operating perturbations show that the advantage is not numerical noise, sample overfitting or an artifact of a favorable assumption. |
| Independent confirmation | Separate construction and acceptance, use suitable independent calculations and a reproducible review package. Same-evaluator replay or agreement between agents alone is insufficient. |

The eventual confirmatory protocol must name the metrics, required physics,
tolerances, technology evidence and decision rule **before** examining its final
comparison. This framework invents no percentage threshold and does not relax or
retroactively redefine historical gates. Useful exploratory and unsolicited work
remains welcome without already satisfying MS1.

“Better” is a design-level statement under explicit constraints, not a single
proxy. A material benefit might concern achievable performance, manufacturability,
robustness or cost, but must be supported by models and evidence adequate for that
claim, with the other requirements and trade-offs made explicit. We cannot certify
undisclosed design choices or unsupported technology assumptions. If the available
reference cannot support this comparison, MS1 remains open; progress on other
public references can still be valuable.

## What we have now, and what we do not

- Steps 1/2 provide bounded local reference tools and reproducible iteration.
- Step 3 establishes a metric-specific improvement over an open Goodman vacuum
  reference, **not** over Proxima's design.
- Step 4 has geometrically acceptable starting coils but no new physically
  accepted coil design; broader physics and engineering remain open.
- The portable public starter checks sparse fixed-current fields and report
  replay. It cannot establish MS1, full SQuID-C readiness or plant performance.

The [SQuID-C intake/readiness documents](README.md) retain their original scope
and failures. They are relevant preparation, not proof that SQuID-C is today's
correct Proxima comparison target. Earlier availability searches are dated records,
not current proof that required data is unavailable.

## Handoff and outreach

When the evidence supports the trigger, prepare a concise technical comparison,
versioned candidate and baseline, reproduction instructions, independent checks,
uncertainties, negative findings and explicit open questions. Record the evidence
review and the actual outreach separately: “comparison supported” and “contact
sent” are different facts. This documentation update sends no message and implies
no affiliation. Existing draft data requests remain unsent historical preparation.

After MS1, use expert feedback to challenge and improve the design and enable
practical follow-on work. The public-benefit goal is larger than outperforming one
reference or securing one organization's recognition.
