## Scientific qualifications to the review

The 2,700-fold gap is normal-RMS divided by the pilot limit, not the whole field
being 2,700 times wrong. The constant-rate round-count illustration does not prove
that another direction, algorithm, start or coil family cannot work. Stopping an
unproductive line of research is a resource decision, not an impossibility result.

The five archived LPQA raw-flux failures use another target, scale, geometry
profile and metric. They are not five known-good designs tested against the
complete Goodman Step 4 profile. Analytical/numerical positive controls already
exist; what is missing is a relevant end-to-end physical calibration. No automatic
threshold relaxation follows from failed reference tests. Local data inspection
does not establish global data unavailability.

These distinctions were checked against the original coil protocols and archived
reconstruction reports by a separate read-only agent; the retained note is
