# Useful contributions

[Programme](STEP4_RESEARCH_PROGRAMME.md) · [Contributing](../../CONTRIBUTING.md)

Choose work that answers a question or removes a concrete reproduction barrier:

- Reproduce a supplied candidate, report both public errors and use the dense
  boundary diagnostic to look for sparse-sampling artifacts.
- Test whether better target physics survives in actual coil fields: interior
  fidelity, magnetic surfaces and the matched Step 3 diagnostic.
- Compare optimizer conditioning with the existing objective, geometry headroom
  and matched time budget. A success flag does not establish an optimum.
- Export the missing interior targets with attribution and verify the existing
  checks on another machine. Assess community benchmark integration first.
- Identify a reactor feasibility constraint that could rule out the current
  direction, with explicit assumptions and a cheap reproducible check.

These are invitations, not an allowlist. Other designs, methods, negative results
and critical reviews are welcome. Compute spending is optional to disclose.
The [portable quickstart](../validation/PUBLIC_QUICKSTART.md) needs no installation;
[native research](README.md) needs its documented environment and artifacts.
Never improve a candidate's score by changing its verifier or dropping failures.
