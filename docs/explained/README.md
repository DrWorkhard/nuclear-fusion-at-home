# The project explained at five levels

The same project, explained five times with increasing depth: what we are doing
and where we stand. Start at any level; each one assumes a little more background.
The format follows the "five levels of difficulty" style of science explanation.
Updated 5 October 2026.

| Level | For | Covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [1. Child](LEVEL_1_CHILD.md) | Anyone, no background | Fusion, a magnetic bottle, and why we are not finished |
| [2. Teenager](LEVEL_2_TEENAGER.md) | School science | Plasma, tokamaks versus stellarators, our two-part puzzle and the roadmap |
| [3. University student](LEVEL_3_UNIVERSITY.md) | Physics, engineering or computing studies | Flux surfaces, quasi-isodynamic fields, coil fitting, our metrics and the public starter |
| [4. Graduate student](LEVEL_4_GRADUATE.md) | Plasma physics or optimization research | Configuration, fitter, acceptance checks, results, lessons and open problems |
| [5. Expert](LEVEL_5_EXPERT.md) | Stellarator researchers and reviewers | Claims and non-claims, safeguards, weaknesses to probe and the next decision |

These pages explain; they do not add evidence. If a number here disagrees with
the [status page](../STATUS.md), the status page and its linked evidence win.
Update all five levels when the project's assessment changes.
